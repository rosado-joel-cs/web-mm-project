Our story, our community

Masjid Muhammad has grown alongside the families it serves — here's who we are and how we got here.

Who we are

Masjid Muhammad is a community mosque in Atlantic City, New Jersey, open to Muslims of every background and to neighbors who want to learn more about Islam. We host the five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, a weekend Islamic school, and programs that support families through every stage of life.

Our community is led by a small team of teachers and volunteers who believe the masjid should be a home — a place people turn to for worship, for learning, and for each other.

Our mission

To provide a welcoming space for worship, Islamic education, and community support — rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah, and open to all.

Worship

Keeping our doors open for prayer, every day, for anyone who walks in.

Education

Teaching Qur'an, Arabic, and Islamic studies to children and adults alike.

Service

Meeting the needs of our community — spiritually, financially, and emotionally.
