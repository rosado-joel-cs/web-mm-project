Rooted in this community since day one

Masjid Muhammad has been a place of prayer, learning, and gathering for Muslim families in Atlantic City. Our doors are open to worshippers, newcomers to Islam, and neighbors of every background.

  • Open for all five daily prayers, every day of the year
  • Weekend Islamic school for children and adults
  • Nikah, funeral, and counseling services for the community
  • Zakat and Sadaqah distribution to families in need
