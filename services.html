Services for every stage of life

Masjid Muhammad supports our community well beyond the prayer hall — from daily worship to major life events.

Worship

Five Daily Prayers & Jumu'ah

The masjid is open for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha every day, with Friday khutbah and congregational prayer weekly.

Education

Weekend Islamic School

Qur'an memorization, Arabic, and Islamic studies classes for children and adults, taught by our resident teachers.

Life Events

Nikah Services

Marriage counseling, officiating, and contract services for couples preparing for their nikah.

Life Events

Janazah & Funeral Support

Ghusl arrangements, janazah prayer, and guidance for families during a loss, coordinated with local cemeteries.

Support

Community Counseling

Confidential guidance for individuals and families navigating personal, marital, or spiritual challenges.

Support

Zakat & Sadaqah Assistance

Distribution of zakat funds to families in need, along with emergency sadaqah support year-round.

Community

Community Events

Iftar gatherings, holiday programs, youth nights, and seasonal events that bring the community together.

Education

New Muslim Support

One-on-one mentorship and introductory classes for those who have recently accepted Islam.

Facility

Hall Rental

Our community hall is available to book for weddings, aqiqah celebrations, and family gatherings.

Need one of these services?

Reach out and a member of our team will get back to you within a day.
